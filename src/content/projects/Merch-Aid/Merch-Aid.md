Merch Aid was a scrappy, fast-moving fundraising platform built during the early months of COVID—when the future of local small businesses (especially in NYC) was suddenly in real danger. The idea was straightforward: help shuttered businesses generate urgent cash to cover rent and wages by selling merchandise people would actually want to wear—created in collaboration with artists and designers.

## The problem

In April 2020, the stakes were painfully clear: many NYC small businesses weren’t going to make it through lockdown. Merch Aid set out to create a new revenue stream for these neighborhood spots—one that could move quickly, scale beyond a single community, and send **100% of profits directly back to the business owners.**

![Merch Aid](/project-images/merch-aid/merch-aid-casamag.png)

## What we built (and how it worked)

Merch Aid paired **mom-and-pop businesses** with **artists and designers** to create exclusive, limited merchandise for each business. The drops were intentionally time-based and repeatable: **new collaborations launched daily at 5PM**, creating a simple rhythm people could follow and show up for.

The roster of collaborators included names like **Paula Scher, Timothy Goodman, Debbie Millman, and New York Nico**, alongside many others—each teaming up with a small business to create something special and worth rallying around.

Merch Aid started in NYC and quickly expanded beyond it, supporting small businesses in places like **LA and Austin** as well.

![Merch Aid](/project-images/merch-aid/merch-aid-labon.png)

## Launch + results

The response was immediate. Drops routinely sold out within hours, and social sharing became a huge driver—people posting what they bought and shouting out the places they were trying to keep alive.

The platform:

- Raised **$500K+** for small businesses
- Reached **top 5% of Shopify stores** in sales during the first two months
- Returned **100% of profits** to the businesses themselves

![Merch Aid](/project-images/merch-aid/merch-aid-ugc.png)

## My role

I joined shortly after the initial launch and jumped in wherever the work was hardest and most urgent.

- Managed outreach to potential partner artists and benefiting small businesses
- Coordinated art production end-to-end
- Worked through merchandise manufacturing logistics
- Produced go-to-market assets for each daily drop (site + social)
- Helped manage Shopify orders and customer support

It was genuinely bootstrapped—high output, lots of moving pieces, and a team doing whatever it took to keep the engine running day after day.

![Merch Aid](/project-images/merch-aid/merch-aid-nynico.png)

## Coverage + awards

The project earned press coverage across outlets including **Vogue, NBC, ABC, GQ, Gothamist, Time Out, and Fast Company**, and it received recognition across major award shows, including:

- **The One Show** (Silver, Bronze, 3x Shortlist)
- **D&AD** (Wood Pencil, Shortlist)
- **Clio Awards** (3x Bronze)
- **Cannes Lions** (Shortlist)
- **ADC** (2x Silver, Bronze)
