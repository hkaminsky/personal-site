At Work & Co, I led a cross-functional team of designers and engineers to redesign and <a href="https://work.co/news/the-new-gatorade-com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">rebuild Gatorade.com</a> from the ground up—moving the brand to a modern ecommerce stack and turning the site into a true direct-to-consumer destination. 

The work spanned far beyond “a new storefront.” We helped Gatorade sharpen how they show up DTC (vs. wholesale), built a new merchandising and education strategy, launched new revenue-driving commerce features, introduced multiple customization experiences (including an AI-powered bottle creator built with Adobe Firefly), created an exclusive Drops program for super-fans, and launched Gatorade’s loyalty program.

## The challenge

Gatorade has massive brand awareness, but building a DTC business requires a different kind of clarity:

- **Why buy direct?** The site needed a reason to exist beyond what customers can get everywhere else.
- **How do you merchandise “beyond the core”?** A broader assortment brings more complexity: discovery, education, and decision-making matter more.
- **How do you keep it fast, premium, and scalable?** The experience needed to feel modern and high-performing—especially as customization and personalization grew in complexity.

## What I led

### 1) A new DTC strategy + streamlined assortment

We started by clarifying Gatorade.com’s role in the ecosystem and shaping the offering around products that differentiate direct: reusable bottles, powders, pods, chews, tablets, equipment, and customization—supported by a content and merchandising approach built for education and conversion.

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/1-gatorade-streamlined-assortment.png)

### 2) Commerce features designed to lift AOV + UPT

We launched multiple features to increase basket-building and make purchasing “systems” (not single items) feel natural:

- Bundling and add-ons
- Upsells designed around customer intent
- A “recirculation” approach that pairs bottles with enhancers and refills
- Bulk ordering through a Team Shop experience
- Gift cards

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/2-gatorade-incentive-construct.png)

### 3) Drops — limited-edition releases for super-fans

To make “buying direct” feel meaningfully different, we launched an exclusive **Drops** program: limited-quantity product and merchandise releases available only while inventory lasts.

We built the experience to support launch moments, scarcity-driven urgency, and collaboration storytelling—helping Gatorade show up more like a culture brand when the moment calls for it.

Drops included collaborations and releases with names like **Caitlin Clark, Eric Emanuel, DJ Khaled, Luka Dončić, Stranger Things, Kristin Juszczyk, the Florida Gators**, and more.

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/3-gatorade-khaled-drop.jpg)

### 4) Merchandising + education grounded in sports science

We built product landing pages and PDP experiences that aren’t just “spec sheets”—they’re built for understanding, confidence, and performance context.

That included introducing GSSI (Gatorade Sports Science Institute) research and athlete performance storytelling directly into the shopping experience, so products are framed around *when to use them, why they work, and what they’re for.*

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/4-gatorade-merchandising.png)

### 5) Fuel Plan — guided recommendations for athletes

We created a Fuel Plan experience that helps athletes answer a few questions about their training and nutrition behaviors, then receive tailored Gatorade product recommendations—plus guidance on how and when to use them relative to workouts.

This was a key moment of moving from “catalog” to “coaching.”

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/5-gatorade-fuel-plan.png)

### 6) Customization experiences (and pushing the ceiling with AI)

We launched a set of customization products that let customers express identity—not just pick a SKU.

**Custom Bottle + Custom Towel**  
Customers can create their own designs using color and pattern selection, pre-made mascots, custom text (with font options), and logo uploads.

**Gatorade AI Bottle (Adobe Firefly-powered)**  
We took customization to the next level with an <a href="https://work.co/news/gatorade-launches-generative-ai-powered-bottle-design-experience/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AI experience</a> where customers type a prompt and generate a design that appears directly on a purchasable bottle.

Bringing this to life meant solving for more than UI polish:

- Content moderation and security considerations
- Performance constraints and load-time implications
- Prompt enrichment to drive consistent, high-quality outputs
- Brand context + prompt engineering: what does “feels like Gatorade” actually mean—and how do you translate that into an AI system?

The launch earned major industry recognition, and I had the opportunity to bring the experience to SXSW—presenting and demoing it live for festival-goers and industry folks.

### 7) Gatorade iD — loyalty program strategy + execution

We launched **Gatorade iD**, Gatorade’s loyalty program, and I led both strategy and delivery: positioning, experience design, and end-to-end implementation.

We implemented Oracle CrowdTwist and designed a custom program experience including a dedicated hub where members can learn the program, earn points, and redeem rewards.

A key unlock was launching **omnichannel earning**—members can upload receipts from any retailer that sells Gatorade and earn points on Gatorade.com, turning everyday purchases into a relationship that continues directly with the brand.

![AI Bottle](/project-images/gatorade-images/7-gatorade-gid.png)
