At R/GA, Samsung was my primary client for four years. I worked as a producer alongside designers, strategists, copywriters, animators, and creative technologists to concept, plan, and deliver global marketing work around Samsung flagship device launches.

The pace was intense and high-volume—spanning digital advertising, TV, partnerships, IRL activations, and e-commerce—often all running in parallel. The project I’m most proud of from that chapter is **Invasion #withGalaxy**, an official **Samsung x Star Wars** partnership that turned the underused Samsung Members app into a must-open experience.

## The challenge

Every Samsung Galaxy phone comes with the Samsung Members app preloaded, but very few customers ever open it—making it difficult to build habit, loyalty, or meaningful engagement through the channel.

At the same time, the bar for Star Wars fandom is brutal: if the experience didn’t feel cinematic and authentic, it would get rejected instantly.

## What we built

We created an **official Star Wars AR experience** that lived **natively inside the Samsung Members app**, timed to the release window of *Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker*.

Users could discover and view film-accurate ships in AR—bringing a “this is happening right here, right now” feeling to everyday spaces and fueling the kind of social sharing that creates real FOMO.

![Invasion](/project-images/samsung-images/samsung-falcon.png)

## How we approached it

We were provided ship models, textures, and sound from the Star Wars production ecosystem—then did the hard work of adapting Hollywood-level fidelity into a real-time mobile AR experience.

That meant:
- Optimizing massive, film-quality assets to run smoothly on a phone
- Recreating ship animations so the spacecraft felt alive in AR
- Triggering authentic sound moments and adding effects (like thruster exhaust)
- Using dynamic light estimation so the ships visually “sat” in the user’s environment instead of floating like stickers

## The rollout

We launched at opening weekend with a **geofenced debut at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles**, then expanded ship-by-ship to select cities across the U.S.

Once the invasion started showing up on social, demand outpaced the plan—people wanted the ships in their cities too—so we **lifted the geofence** and let the experience scale.

![Invasion](/project-images/samsung-images/samsung-map.png)

## Impact

Invasion #withGalaxy led to a **36% increase in daily active users** for Samsung Members during the campaign window.

![Invasion](/project-images/samsung-images/samsung-ugc.png)

## My role

As producer, I was the connective tissue across creative, technology, and delivery—helping turn an ambitious concept into something shippable, on time, and worthy of both Samsung and Star Wars. That included:
- Orchestrating cross-discipline production (creative, animation, tech, strategy, copy)
- Managing a high-stakes timeline tied to a major entertainment release
- Protecting quality while navigating real constraints (performance, file size, device variability)
- Driving stakeholder alignment so the experience felt official—not “promotional”
