Before product, I was a writer and editor. I contributed stories to Digital Trends and IEEE GlobalSpec on nights and weekends, covering breaking tech news and emerging trends. In college, I served as editor-in-chief and later managing editor of The Pitt News, my daily student newspaper. There, I led a team of 80+ student journalists, launched our digital department, redesigned the paper’s print and web presence, and helped shift the newsroom into a digital-first era. That early foundation sharpened my instincts for storytelling, editing, and fast-paced decision-making—skills I still draw on every day in product work.
